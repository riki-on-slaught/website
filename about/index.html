✳ My introduction

Hello, I'm Sharad.

I'm a chocolatier, graphic design enthusiast, and AI prompt engineer based in Lalitpur, Nepal. I'm motivated by thoughtful work: learning the details of a craft, finding a clear way to express an idea, and working with people to make something better. Two years of international experience in Dubai helped strengthen my adaptability and gave me a wider perspective on collaboration and quality.

✳ My journey

Where it began

My story starts in Nepal, where I built my educational foundation and began developing the practical skills, care, and curiosity I bring to my work today.

International experience

Learning in Dubai

Two years in the chocolate industry in Dubai gave me experience in a fast-paced, multicultural setting. Precision, quality awareness, teamwork, and adaptability became part of my everyday approach.

The next chapter

Growing through creativity

Back in Nepal, I'm building on that hands-on foundation while exploring graphic design and AI prompt engineering—two fields where visual thinking and clear communication can meet.
